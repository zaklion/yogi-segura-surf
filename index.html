FAQ

Questions about the online surf course

Is this for complete beginners?

Yes. Module 0 lets you pick a beginner or intermediate track, and every lesson starts with a land drill before the water demo.

I live far from the ocean. Will it help?

Yes. About 40% of the course is land and home drills, like pop-up drills you can do in a hotel room, that speed up progress before your next trip.

How long do I have access?

Forever, including future updates. One payment, no subscription.

Is there live coaching or calls?

No. That's by design: it's self-paced. Want personal feedback? The Video Analysis tier gives you two recorded breakdowns from Yogi, each delivered within 7 days of uploading your clips.

What do I need?

A phone or laptop. A board is optional for the land drills.

Can I gift it?

Yes, through the gift option at checkout.

Can I join a trip with Yogi?

Join the free Costa Rica coaching week waitlist. Course owners get first access and a credit.

What if it's not for me?

You're covered by the "First Session" 30-Day Guarantee. Email us within 30 days for a full refund, processed through Gumroad. No questions, no calls.

How do I get access after I buy?

Checkout and delivery are handled by Gumroad. You get an email with instant access to stream every lesson on any device.
